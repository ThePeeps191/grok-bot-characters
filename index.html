Meet Grok Bot characters

Characters drawn entirely in code, recreated from the Grok Bot beta's demo footage. There are 22 shapes and 20 colors, and every shape can morph into every other one. Each bot blinks, glances around, bounces, spits dots, types, works inside orbiting trails, and turns into a “!” when it needs you.
